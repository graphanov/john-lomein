# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-focused.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and remove personal or confidential information from reports.
- Accept correction, acknowledge impact, and repair harm.
- Keep disagreements relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate misrepresentation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Exposing credentials, private memory, raw logs, hidden model reasoning, or private repository data.
- Using project channels to bypass owner, moderation, security, merge, release, or publishing boundaries.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads, chat spaces, events, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or moderation team. Include only the information needed to investigate; do not repeat sensitive content in public.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behavior is unsafe or incompatible with this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and protect reporter confidentiality where possible.

Good-faith reports will not be punished. Deliberately false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this code.
